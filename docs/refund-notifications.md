# Refund Request Notifications (#39)

`submitRefundRequest` commits the existing request, captures the current Platform Admin audience and event facts synchronously, then independently attempts the buyer confirmation and admin opening before sending the existing email. The buyer is the opening actor; an actor who is also a Platform Admin does not receive their own opening. Promotion and demotion affect subsequent openings without role sync or historical replay.

`submitRefundDecision` calls the existing synchronous approval or decline transaction. A successful decision produces one buyer update before the existing email. The recipient is the Purchase buyer, including Team Licences; the actor is the deciding Platform Admin. Rejected, repeated and rolled-back decisions produce no update or email. Approval wording describes the decision without claiming a payout. Declines include the committed note and explain that another request may be raised.

All delivery uses the shared Cadence `deliverNotification` and injected `NotificationTransport`. Event keys are `cadence:<kind>:<refundRequestId>`, with separate confirmation, opening, approval and decline kinds. Snapshot facts preserve integer-cent amounts and Cadence-composed text. Actor avatars come from synced person identities through the shared feed adapter. No earlier opening is archived, rewritten or marked read by a decision. Delivery failures are logged and caught; email exceptions retain their prior behavior after required Notification attempts.

Opening destinations use `/admin/refunds?refundRequestId=<id>#refund-request-<id>`. The existing Platform Admin authorization runs before validating or looking up the target. The loader chooses its current status, so retained opening links still resolve after approval or decline. Status tabs clear the target parameter. Buyer updates use `/settings#refund-request-<id>`, where history remains scoped to the session buyer. Both pages render the exact anchor; notification links grant no additional authority.

The three workflow definitions under `notifications/workflows/` use `cadence-refund-request-opened`, `cadence-refund-request-approved` and `cadence-refund-request-declined`. Opening uses `open-refund-requests`; both decisions share `refund-request-updates` with confirmation. Each has one feed step and `override_preferences: false`. Muting feed categories does not change existing email behavior.

`KNOCK_FEED_CHANNEL_KEY=cadence-feed npm run notifications:provision -- --dry-run` validates definitions and prints their proposed category-catalog and Development workflow writes. It does not publish or activate workflows. See [the provisioning procedure](notification-feed.md#publish-all-eight-workflows-in-development) for actual deployment.

Controlled checks use migrated SQLite, the shared fake transport and real session-backed route actions. They cover current audiences, actor exclusion, snapshot timing, preference suppression, independent failures, committed entitlements, Team Licence buyers, stable event identities, rejected/repeated decisions, exact rendered destinations and existing authorization. Existing refund domain tests remain the regression checks for progress preservation, separately held Claimed Seats, all-or-nothing Team Licence refunds and integer-cent arithmetic.

The [2026-11-18 live checks](notification-live-verification.md) verified real Individual Purchase opening/approval/decline actions, Team Licence confirmation with its full amount, two Admin recipients, duplicate/invalid rejection, local email outbox entries, exact destinations, and retained opening links after a decision. All Refund Request workflows also suppressed delivery when their categories were muted. The full acceptance contract still includes all role/holding combinations and failure timings; recent-item checks do not establish aged-history guarantees.
