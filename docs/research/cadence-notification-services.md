# Hosted in-app notifications for Cadence

Research date: **2026-11-16**. Resolves [Which hosted notification services could fit Cadence?](https://github.com/bzhang-coke/cohort-005-project/issues/3), under [Plan Cadence in-app notifications](https://github.com/bzhang-coke/cohort-005-project/issues/1).

This is a documentation and SDK-source comparison of **Knock and Novu Cloud**, one credible hosted alternative. Both document a persistent recipient inbox, real-time delivery, customizable React UI, and backend APIs. Neither is selected. No account was created, SDK installed, service called, or Cadence integration tested. “Documented” below means vendor documentation or source establishes the capability; “inference” means an integration implication; “trial” means evidence still needed. Prices and limits reflect pages opened during this research, not a contractual quote or a forecast of Cadence traffic.

## Cadence fit

The starting point is `979e285`: React Router 8.3 / React 19, SSR on Node, a sidebar shell, SQLite/Drizzle, numeric session user IDs, and DevUI account/country switching. The relevant local evidence is [package.json](../../package.json), [SSR configuration](../../react-router.config.ts), [session handling](../../app/lib/session.ts), [app layout](../../app/routes/layout.app.tsx), and [DevUI](../../app/components/dev-ui.tsx). The [README](../../README.md) names Knock as a course candidate, which is not a provider decision. Existing refund email remains separate and outside this investigation.

**Conditional assessment:** Knock merits a trial if broad free-tier workflow coverage and long-lived inbox history matter, and its paid entry price is acceptable. Novu Cloud merits a trial if a smaller paid entry price matters and its workflow/history limits fit the eventual catalogue. Both support custom layouts, so the sidebar does not force a provider or a top-header bell. These are inferences from the evidence below, not recommendations to purchase.

Students, Instructors, Team Admins, and Platform Admins can all map to individual provider recipients. Cadence must determine recipient eligibility from its own data; vendor dashboard roles are not Cadence application permissions. A Team Admin is also a Student, so separate role inbox identities could duplicate notifications. Decide whether role/context is just a filter before introducing extra identities or provider tenancy. A country switch is not automatically a tenant boundary. These are design implications of Cadence's [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) and session model; the event/recipient catalogue remains open. A Refund Request decision and confirmation of an Issued Refund remain distinct events.

## Free tier and paid path

All amounts are USD per month. In-app feed messages are the scope; guides, AI features, other channels, taxes, and negotiated discounts are excluded.

| Dimension         | Knock                                                                                              | Novu Cloud                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Free allowance    | 10,000 delivered messages; unlimited workflows, profiles, team members, and environments.          | 10,000 workflow runs; **5 workflows**, 2 environments, 3 team members; unlimited subscribers.                                  |
| First paid plan   | Starter: $250 including 50,000 messages; $0.005 per additional message. Branding removal included. | Pro: starts at $30 including 30,000 runs; $1.20 per additional 1,000; 20 workflows, 2 environments. Branding removal included. |
| Further paid path | Enterprise: custom volume or monthly-notified-user agreement.                                      | Team: starts at $250 including 250,000 runs, same listed overage unit, up to 10 environments; Enterprise custom.               |

Sources: [Knock pricing](https://knock.app/pricing), [Novu pricing](https://www.novu.co/pricing/). The opened Novu pricing page says **5** free workflows; an older search result said 20. Use the opened page and reconfirm the account's actual entitlement before choosing.

Knock counts each successful recipient/channel message, including non-production delivery. Suppressed messages do not count. Its in-app channel cannot use the generic channel sandbox mode; development sends can consume allowance. [Knock usage rules](https://docs.knock.app/manage-your-account/knock-plans)

Novu counts one workflow execution per subscriber, across all environments, regardless of steps/channels. A topic fan-out therefore consumes a run per included subscriber. Free stops new runs at 10,000 with HTTP 402; paid plans allow overages by default unless a configured usage limit pauses them. [Novu billing](https://docs.novu.co/platform/account/billing)

Knock's free-limit FAQ says it does not hard-stop messages and will contact the customer about billing. Treat the listed allowance as the free entitlement, not a promise of unlimited free overage. [Knock pricing FAQ](https://knock.app/pricing#frequently-asked-questions)

Illustration: assume every subscriber workflow produces **one** delivered in-app item; no batching, skips, retries billed as new work, or other chargeable features; totals include development. Then delivered items and subscriber runs are equal. These are arithmetic examples, not Cadence demand estimates:

| Monthly items/runs | Knock                             | Novu Cloud                         |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 5,000              | $0, within free limits            | $0, if all free feature limits fit |
| 30,000             | $250 Starter                      | $30 base Pro                       |
| 100,000            | $250 + 50,000 × $0.005 = **$500** | $30 + 70 × $1.20 = **$114**        |

Calculations use the [Knock rate card](https://knock.app/pricing) and [Novu rate card](https://www.novu.co/pricing/). Sending one event to 100 recipients means 100 items/runs under these assumptions. Batching can break this equivalence: Knock meters resulting messages, while Novu meters subscriber executions. Recalculate from the agreed catalogue and confirm how digested runs appear in the account's usage before extrapolating savings.

## Node, React 19, and SSR evidence

Knock documents its `@knocklabs/node` server SDK and `@knocklabs/react`/`@knocklabs/client` UI stack. The React package at source commit `81b9db8` declares React/ReactDOM 17, 18, or 19 peer support. This establishes declared React 19 compatibility, not a tested published release in Cadence. [Server SDKs](https://docs.knock.app/developer-tools/sdks), [React manifest snapshot](https://github.com/knocklabs/javascript/blob/81b9db8f0f8dc1f2a9af7bd110917845034b7f03/packages/react/package.json)

Novu documents `@novu/api` backend triggers and explicitly supports React 19. The `@novu/react` manifest at source commit `0cfc48f` declares React 18/19 peers and distinct browser/server exports. Its Next.js example uses a client component; that is relevant SSR evidence, not a React Router compatibility guarantee. [Backend trigger](https://docs.novu.co/platform/workflow/trigger-workflow), [React support](https://docs.novu.co/platform/inbox), [manifest snapshot](https://github.com/novuhq/novu/blob/0cfc48fe2995361f0a64ca328efae8b190ce1ddd/packages/react/package.json), [Next.js example](https://docs.novu.co/platform/quickstart/nextjs)

**Trial required for a provisional candidate:** pin published packages, import/build/render under Cadence's actual SSR setup, hydrate, navigate, and switch accounts. Check browser-only initialization, stylesheet behavior, and the notification client lifecycle. No claim is made that either SDK currently passes Cadence's typecheck/build/runtime. A client-rendered notification region is an available design option to evaluate if full SSR causes difficulty; it is not yet an agreed implementation.

## Identity and isolation

Knock's enhanced security mode is off by default. Without it, a public key can act as arbitrary users. Enable it and issue backend-signed RS256 JWTs with the recipient in `sub` and an expiry; access grants can narrow capabilities and tenant access. The public key is not a user credential. [Knock security](https://docs.knock.app/in-app-ui/security-and-authentication)

Novu similarly warns that an application identifier plus a guessed subscriber ID can expose the feed without HMAC protection. Enable HMAC and calculate `HMAC-SHA256(secretKey, subscriberId)` on the server. Context-scoped inboxes also need a signed context; do not trust arbitrary client context input. [Novu production security](https://docs.novu.co/platform/inbox/prepare-for-production)

**Cadence integration inference:** derive the recipient ID from the verified session and existing user record, consistently stringify the numeric ID, and return only that recipient's credential. An unauthenticated request must not receive one. Do not accept a requested user ID from the browser as authority. Keep secret API/signing keys server-side. The credential issuer ultimately depends on Cadence's authentication; DevUI switching demonstrates integration behavior, not production login security. Reinitialize or clear the previous feed/client on switching or logout so stale rows, counts, and sockets cannot cross identities. Knock documents an `enabled` gate that disconnects and clears data; the complete account-switch behavior still needs testing. [Knock provider lifecycle](https://docs.knock.app/in-app-ui/react/sdk/components/knock-provider)

Tenant filtering also needs deliberate design. Novu requires the trigger and Inbox to have the same set of context keys; an unscoped Inbox does not automatically aggregate scoped notifications. A generic role/team filter should not inadvertently hide events or become an authorization boundary. [Novu Inbox context matching](https://docs.novu.co/platform/inbox/configuration/inbox-with-context)

**Architecture inference:** neither provider atomically commits a Cadence SQLite change and its hosted trigger. The integration plan must decide which events tolerate loss versus require a durable dispatch record/retry mechanism, how recipient lists are resolved, and how duplicates after the provider's deduplication window are prevented. Provider retries cover only work the provider receives. Conversely, successful HTTP acceptance does not prove a visible inbox item. Re-fetch the persisted feed after reconnect; test cross-tab state reconciliation. Do not conflate archiving, reading, and completing a business action, and recheck Cadence authorization at the linked destination.

## Retention, deletion, and data minimization

Knock keeps feed items available indefinitely even after diagnostic message/run logs expire: 30 days on non-Enterprise plans, generally 90 days on Enterprise; API logs remain 30 days. A delete-user API promises removal of the user and associated data. Archive only hides feed items. Pricing describes configurable feed retention, while the retention guide says custom policies are unsupported: confirm the actual feed-specific expiry/purge mechanism if bounded storage is required. [Retention](https://docs.knock.app/manage-your-account/data-retention), [delete user](https://docs.knock.app/api-reference/users/delete), [feed retention listing](https://knock.app/pricing)

Novu distinguishes **Inbox history** (Free 30 days; Pro/Team 90 days) from **Activity Feed logs** (24 hours / 7 days / 90 days). Enterprise allows custom retention. Notification delete/deleteAll methods are documented; broader specific-data deletion may require support. Do not assume notification deletion proves immediate erasure from backups or every log. [Retention](https://docs.novu.co/platform/additional-resources/security), [notification deletion](https://docs.novu.co/platform/sdks/javascript)

Send minimal IDs, display content, and safe action references. Knock defaults to returning trigger data and recipient/actor properties in its feed, with response filters available; Novu likewise exposes trigger payload in the feed. Therefore server-only/private fields should not be embedded in notification payloads by default. [Knock response filtering](https://docs.knock.app/integrations/in-app/feeds), [Novu payload contract](https://docs.novu.co/api-reference/events/trigger-event)

No region constraint was supplied. If one emerges, Novu documents US/EU availability and region-bound keys; Knock currently describes EU residency as forthcoming rather than available. Resolve the required region before provisioning. [Novu regions](https://docs.novu.co/platform/additional-resources/security), [Knock region note](https://docs.knock.app/manage-your-account/knock-plans)

## Remaining decisions and proportionate trial

The existing catalogue/UI/provider decisions should settle the first-release roles and events, recipient counts/fan-out, badge semantics, grouping, required history, acceptable branding, and paid-price threshold. This research does not choose them, invent a traffic forecast, or require trials of both services.

A now-specifiable prerequisite question is: **“Can the provisionally preferred hosted service satisfy Cadence's identity isolation and SSR/inbox behavior under its actual account entitlements?”** Run this only after a provisional choice and agreed representative behavior; revisit the choice if it fails. Evidence required before declaring the integration plan ready:

1. **Account and limits:** confirm free/paid entitlements, retention and region fit; for Novu, confirm idempotency is enabled if reliable retries are required. Ask for clarification of custom retention/deletion only if it affects the chosen requirements.
2. **Runtime and isolation:** pinned SDK versions in the existing SSR stack; successful authenticated feed for two distinct recipients; rejection of a forged recipient; no stale data after DevUI switching/logout; server secrets absent from client output.
3. **Agreed core behavior:** one representative server-triggered notification, refresh/reconnect persistence, the chosen seen/read/archive semantics, and a duplicate trigger. Add a second tab, expired-token behavior, fan-out, or grouping only where the plan relies on them. Inspect Novu's cached-error behavior rather than assuming every retry reexecutes a failed request.

Useful later verification, rather than automatic blockers to choosing a provider: exhaustive mobile/accessibility testing, sustained load/cost measurements, broader failure injection, and every optional UI feature. Any such item becomes a prerequisite only when the agreed first-release requirements depend on it. No signup, enablement request, or runtime trial was performed as part of this research.
